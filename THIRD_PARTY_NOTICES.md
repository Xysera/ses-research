# Third-party material and license boundaries

The repository uses a split license:

- Original software authored for this project: Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE`).
- Manuscript, original documentation, original figures, and original research data authored for this project: CC BY 4.0 (`LICENSE-DOCUMENTATION.md`).

These licenses do **not** relicense third-party material. In particular:

- Benchmark questions, answer choices, and dataset-derived content remain subject to the licenses or terms of their source datasets.
- Released Taylor et al. auditing-game logs and related artifacts remain subject to the upstream repository or dataset terms.
- Model names, model cards, model weights, tokenizer files, and quantized model artifacts remain subject to their publishers' terms. Model weights are not redistributed here.
- Papers, abstracts, quotations, downloaded source text, and bibliographic metadata remain subject to applicable copyright and source terms; their inclusion is for research verification and citation.
- Third-party software dependencies remain under their respective licenses.

Users who redistribute or build on these materials are responsible for checking the applicable upstream terms. Where a file contains both original analysis and third-party source material, CC BY 4.0 applies only to the original contributions to the extent those contributions are separately licensable.
