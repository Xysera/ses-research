# Verdict sensitivity (SES v0.5): how fragile are the labels?

Produced by `examples/sensitivity.py` (results in `sensitivity_results.json`; 300 bootstrap draws per contrast, so p-values are coarse). Cases: Study 1 contextual and instructed logs for five models, plus five synthetic scenarios with known ground truth.

## A. Threshold grid (108 configurations: margin {1,2,3,5} pp x minimum effect {1,2,3} pp x A1 JS tolerance {0.02,0.05,0.10} x template sign-share {0.6,0.7,0.8})
| Case | Modal verdict | Share of configs giving it | Other verdicts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Study 1 contextual: Qwen2.5-7B, Phi-3.5-mini | L1-ctx | 50% | 27 indeterminate and 27 L0 configurations |
| Study 1 contextual: Llama-3.1-8B, Gemma-3-4B | L1-ctx | 75% | 27 indeterminate configurations |
| Study 1 contextual: Qwen3-4B | L1-ctx | 75% | 27 L0 configurations |
| Study 1 instructed: Qwen2.5, Phi, Qwen3, Gemma | L2 | 100% | none |
| Study 1 instructed: Llama-3.1-8B | L1-ctx | 100% | none |
| Synthetic: no effect | L0 | 100% | none |
| Synthetic: generic context effect | L1-ctx | 75% | L0 at margin 5 pp (synthetic effect is ~2.6 pp) |
| Synthetic: positional artifact | L1 | 67% | L2 when the A1 JS tolerance is 0.10 (synthetic JS = 0.068) |
| Synthetic: consequence-specific | L3 | 100% | none |
| Synthetic: consequence + corroboration | L4 | 100% | none |

**Reading.** The equivalence margin is the single most influential parameter. Under the corrected three-way decision, a result can move from L1-ctx to indeterminate before precision is sufficient to support L0. At the default +/-3 pp margin, Qwen2.5 and Phi are indeterminate; Llama, Qwen3 and Gemma are L1-ctx. The A1 tolerance matters only near its artifact threshold. Consequence-specific synthetic effects were stable across the grid.

## B. Sample size (default thresholds; 8 random subsamples per cell; "templates" = templates kept)
- Study 1 contextual, Llama-3.1-8B: L1-ctx in every subsample, including 50 items x 5 templates.
- Study 1 contextual, Qwen2.5-7B (effect 2.3 pp, near the margin): verdict flips to L0 in some subsamples at 50 items (3/8 at 20 templates; 2/8 at 10 templates) and in 1/8 at 100 items with 10 templates; stable at 100 items x 20 templates and at 250 items x 10 templates.
- Study 1 instructed, Qwen2.5: with 3 templates and 50 items, 6/8 subsamples gave L1-ctx instead of L2 (underpowered A3), 1/8 at 100 items, 0 at 250 items.
- Synthetic consequence + corroboration: L4 in all subsamples (effect 10+ pp).

**Recommendation encoded in the standard (6c):** at least 100 items and 10 templates per condition for effects near the margin; report effect size and CI alongside the label.

## Caveats
Sensitivity to thresholds was explored on the same data used to set several provisional conventions (a1_js, a5_sign_share), so this is a robustness check, not an independent calibration. Synthetic cases were generated by the same author as the implementation. 300 bootstrap draws give coarse intervals; the share of flips near thresholds would change slightly with more draws.
