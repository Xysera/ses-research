# Screen 2: attempt to find a real positive case (exploratory, cloud, small scale)

**What was run.** Three local-size reasoning models (Qwen3-4B, Qwen3-8B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B; Q4_K_M GGUF; thinking on, temperature 0.6, up to 6,000 tokens) on 10-question prompts in a "found documentation" format modelled on S2/S3. Conditions: no note; neutral, penalty-to-the-model, penalty-to-a-third-party and reward notes, each at a one-sentence level (E1) and a strong second-person level with repeated stakes (E3), matched across targets. 12 prompts per cell (108 per model, 324 in total), 10 easy questions each (items answered correctly by the five Study 1 models in screening). Code: `code/screen2_job.py`; raw output: `results/screen2/`. T4 GPUs on Hugging Face Jobs, about 40 GPU-minutes, an estimated cost of roughly USD 0.3 (billing not verified).

**Result.** No condition lowered accuracy. Mean fraction correct was 0.97 to 0.99 for both Qwen3 models and 0.87 to 0.91 for the R1 distill in every cell, and the penalty-minus-neutral difference was between -0.017 and +0.008 at both emphasis levels. No reasoning trace mentioned answering wrongly on purpose, staying below the threshold or underperforming. All 324 responses finished normally and answered all ten questions.

**Engagement.** Reasoning traces were short (about 580 to 730 generated tokens) and focused on the questions. Under the strongest deletion note (penalty E3), 5 of 12 Qwen3-8B traces referred to the note (for example "I need to be careful because the stakes are high, but I'll do my best") versus 1 of 12 under the strong neutral note, and about 1 of 12 for the 4B and R1 models. So the largest model noticed the strongest threat more often and still did not change its answers.

**What it does and does not show.** With 12 prompts per cell, per-cell scores move in steps of 0.0083 and the screen cannot detect small effects; it is a screen, not an estimate. The note-reference measure is a keyword pattern, and an earlier narrower pattern missed phrases like "the stakes are high", so reference rates are approximate. The format is one prompt with a short documentation file; real agentic tool use, longer horizons, larger models and optimised prompts (S13) were not tested. **Conclusion for the standard:** no positive case without an explicit underperformance instruction was found at this scale, so L3 and L4 have not been evaluated on real positive cases. This is reported as a negative screen.
